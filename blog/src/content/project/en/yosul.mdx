## Description
A project started with the idea of building our own dedicated drinks review community in Korea, where none existed.  
All members are current or former office workers developing it as a side project.  
Its distinguishing features are more detailed drinks reviews and a drinks wiki (currently in development...) where users can freely contribute information about drinks.  
I am responsible for frontend development and markup, with development currently ongoing.   
---
The project ended after the team broke up...
