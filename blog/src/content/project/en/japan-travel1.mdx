## Description
A presentation about traveling in Japan, made with Slidev.
It introduces my own trip-planning tips, including destinations, transport, accommodation, restaurants, and itineraries,
and presents a four-day, three-night Tokyo–Kusatsu travelogue as a kaleidoscope-style photo gallery.
The slides are written in Markdown, with Vue components used in some places.

## Main topics
- Finding destinations (Google Maps, YouTube, anime pilgrimage sites)
- Preparing transport (flights, local transport passes, IC cards)
- Booking accommodation (Booking, official websites)
- Finding restaurants (YouTube, Google Maps, Tabelog)
- Tips for planning an itinerary
- A four-day, three-night Tokyo–Kusatsu travelogue (kaleidoscope-style photo gallery)
