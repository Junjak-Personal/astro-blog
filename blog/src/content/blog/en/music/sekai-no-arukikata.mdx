## Impressions

I do not usually gravitate toward such bright, hopeful songs, but this one felt different.

Was it because my favorite, Tsukiyomi, wrote and composed it, or because the vocals mix a yearning quality into an unmistakably bright song?\
It seemed to brighten my gloomy mood on the commute, so I think I listened to it quite a lot.
