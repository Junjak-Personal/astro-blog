## First Impressions

I first tried it during a trip to Yamaguchi Prefecture in spring 2024, when I bought a three-bottle Dassai tasting set at a liquor shop and compared them with a friend. \
The three varieties were 23, 39, and 45; we drank them in the order 45 -&gt; 39 -&gt; 23. \
I do not remember the snacks very well, but I think they were just some simple crackers or something...?

I remember 45 having a somewhat pronounced alcohol presence.

Perhaps because it was the least expensive of the three, it did not leave that great an impression?

A lot of people praise it, but Dassai tends to be so light and clean that, for me, it leaves something to be desired.

<ImageLoader src="/files/media/e85d01837a10fb8b.webp" alt="blog-image" />

## Pairing Notes

Sweet and light-bodied. Perhaps because it is so light and clean, I find it difficult to pin down the flavors clearly.\
With a sake meter value of 3 and acidity of 1.5, perhaps it is a textbook example of a light, clean junmai daiginjo..?
