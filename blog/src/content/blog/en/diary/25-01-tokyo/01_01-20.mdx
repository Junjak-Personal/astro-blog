## Travel Diary  
  
### Travel Purpose  
  
<PolaroidGalleryScrapbook items={purposeItems} />  
  
My friend must have enjoyed our previous Tokyo trip, because they asked me to guide them and their spouse around Tokyo on their honeymoon.  
I was thoroughly unemployed and had nothing much to do, so the idea of tagging along on a trip sounded good. I accepted straight away, and that was how this January Tokyo trip came about.  
Later on, I feel like I overspent and I regret it.  
(While planning the trip, Tesla was doing so well that I felt I could afford to spend a little. Unfortunately, that is no longer the case...)  
  
### Tsukishima monja street  
  
<PolaroidGalleryScrapbook items={monjaStreetItems} />  
  
I found this restaurant because my friend wanted to try monjayaki in Tokyo.  
Visited the most famous Moheji on Tsukishima monja street.  
  
Luckily, I was able to go in and order right away without waiting, but it took a long time to get ready because there were so many people.  
We ordered mentai monja and gomoku monja from the recommended list, along with butter-sauteed shirako.  
They even had Jikon, so I couldn't resist ordering some.  
  
The taste is the most delicious among monja. In particular, it must be said that it was Nurungji, but it was quite delicious to scrape off the dough just before it burned.  
I think it was the price, taste, and service that explains why it maintained a high score of 4.8 points on Google Maps despite having 7,000 reviews.  
  
### Ginza  
  
<PolaroidGalleryScrapbook items={ginzaItems} />  
  
In Ginza, my friend and their spouse and I split up for some time on our own, and I headed to Itoya, the stationery shop I had wanted to visit.  
A stationery store that is fun to browse as it has small items and stationery that stimulate the desire to buy.  
  
In addition to stationery, there were various props to decorate the room, so I couldn't resist and bought a magnet.  
I was very interested in stationery when I was in middle school and high school, but I lost a lot of interest after entering college, but it was fun to see so many different types again.  
  
### Ueno  
  
<PolaroidGalleryScrapbook items={uenoItems} />  
  
We join again in the evening to eat together.  
I made a reservation and visited Yakitori Daio Ueno branch, a yakitori izakaya with a rating of about 3.4 based on Tabelog.  
For a 3.4, the taste was quite disappointing, so I just ordered one assorted skewer and a glass of draft beer each.  
Google wasn't bad at 4.3 either.  
  
Afterwards, I looked around the Ameyoko shopping street, and there were quite a lot of Ameyoko products, and many of them had provocative T-shirts displayed openly, which was a bit embarrassing.  
It was a bit surprising to see so many foreigners (Southeast Asians, Chinese, etc.) doing business.  
  
While looking for where to go for the second round, I found this store: Shinobazu Brewery Hitsuji.  
We ordered grilled lamb, lamb large-intestine stew, and lamb roast beef. The others were fairly ordinary, but the grilled lamb was genuinely delicious.  
When I stopped by a slightly more expensive lamb restaurant, the lamb here was so tender and well-cooked that I ended up ordering just the lamb roast.  
The seasoning was delicious, and there were quite a few types of craft beer, so I was quite satisfied with this restaurant.  
  
I end my day with this. The yakitori I ate at Ameyoko Shopping Street was a bit disappointing, but I was satisfied because the grilled lamb was delicious.
