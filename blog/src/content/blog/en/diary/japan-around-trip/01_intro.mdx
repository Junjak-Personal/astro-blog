# Getting Started  
  
A travel journal of my 27-day journey around Japan, from November 28 to December 24.  
  
I'd vaguely thought about buying a 21-day JR Rail Pass and setting off on a trip. After leaving my job, I started looking up places I wanted to visit and gradually put together a 27-day itinerary.  
  
The rough route I came up with is below.  
The places marked with red numbers were my main overnight bases, or the locations of my accommodation.  
I booked all 26 nights about two months before the trip began.  
The most demanding part of planning was choosing accommodation while checking Shinkansen and local train schedules, routes, and whether I'd be able to arrive on time.  
  
Some stretches around Nagoya and between Tokyo and Sendai make very little sense geographically. I arranged those to meet up with friends coming from Korea...  
Specifically: Kanazawa → Tsuruga → Nagoya, and Aomori → Tokyo → Sendai → Narita International Airport.  
If I'd traveled alone the whole way, I probably would have gone from day six through Kanazawa → Niigata → Akita → Aomori → Sendai → Tokyo → Nagano → Yamanashi → Shizuoka → Nagoya → Wakayama → Osaka...?  
  
<ImageLoader src="/files/blog/diary/japan-around-trip/assets/image-20250103023827664.webp" alt="image-20250103023827664" />  
  
Traveling around Japan was on the bucket list I wrote during military service. I'm finally doing it seven years after being discharged, which feels pretty emotional...  
(I was born in 1996.)  
  
During the trip, I bought keychains from local souvenir shops and charms from shrines and temples and hung them on my crossbody bag. There ended up being rather a lot...  
  
<ImageLoader src="/files/blog/diary/japan-around-trip/assets/image-20250106011733489.webp" alt="image-20250106011733489" />  
  
These are the omikuji fortunes, admission tickets, and other things I collected while traveling.  
At the end of each day, I looked back on the day's travels and pasted them into a notebook, as shown in the photo.  
Looking through it afterward brings back memories and is fun in its own right. I'm glad I did it.  
  
<ImageLoader src="/files/blog/diary/japan-around-trip/assets/image-20250106011724165.webp" alt="image-20250106011724165" />  
  
So, let the journey around Japan begin on November 28.
