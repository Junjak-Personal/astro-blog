### return home  
  
From Sendai City, where I had fallen in love, I took the Shinkansen to Ueno and then the Keisei Line to return to Narita Airport.  
Akihabara duty-free shop at the departure hall after completing formalities  
I don't think there was anything like this the last time I came (2 years ago)...  
Pasola Akihabara duty-free shop was selling quite a lot of various products, including alcohol and goods.  
I'd already bought plenty of sake, and my backpack and hand luggage were full. With hardly any money left either, I just browsed without buying anything.  
  
What I felt as I finished my long journey of 27 nights and 28 days was that, of course, traveling to Japan is fun. I can summarize it in one line.  
I didn't really gain any enlightenment or anything like that during the travel period, I just walked around, ate... I literally went sightseeing.  
I didn't think it was a major turning point in my life because I had a pleasant conversation with the person I met at that time and there was no further connection.  
  
What I strongly thought was that, unlike our country and other regions, each tourist destination has its own strong characteristics, so I found it very interesting to have different tastes.  
Still, there was a lot of story to tell, and the entire trip was fun (even though it was a trip where I spent a lot of money...), so it was a trip I have no regrets about.
