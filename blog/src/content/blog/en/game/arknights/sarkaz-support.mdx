I cleared end3 on difficulty 15 in Sarkaz Integrated Strategies with the Support Squad.  
  
The Wi-shung-jwak picks felt too broken, so I recruited other operators instead.  
After a few failed attempts, I finally picked up Kal'tsit and Civilight Eterna, then made it to the boss without much trouble.  
  
Kal'tsit with the King's set + Civilight Eterna was ridiculously strong.  
Even our man Patriot could barely put up a fight before going down.  
  
She was already good with collectibles in Sami, but the collectible synergies in Sarkaz seem to have made her even better.  
In Sarkaz, she might rank just below healer Eyjafjalla?  
  
That Integrated Strategies-exclusive module offers absurd value...
