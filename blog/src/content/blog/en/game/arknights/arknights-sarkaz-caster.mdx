I cleared end3 on difficulty 15 in Sarkaz Integrated Strategies with the Caster Squad.  
I climbed without the notoriously overpowered picks nicknamed Wi-shung-jwak. Maybe I got lucky with the recruitment vouchers, because I had little trouble getting other strong operators  
and the hand collectibles, which let me put together a pretty busted run.  
  
I tried Virtuosa + Nymph, and they dealt solid damage to Patriot even from more than two tiles away.  
It is not shown in the video, but the era modifier was giving all enemies +50% HP.  
Still, with an overpowered collectible setup, Goldenglow seems stronger than Nymph.
