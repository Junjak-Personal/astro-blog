### Subtitles

It is used in the same way as the track tag inside an HTML5 video element.   
However, setting two or more subtitle languages seems to prevent the subtitle selection menu from loading...   
Subtitles display correctly after a refresh but fail to appear after navigating to the page.  

[track tag documentation](https://developer.mozilla.org/ko/docs/Web/HTML/Element/track)


[Zorn issue link](https://ark.sudovanilla.org/MinPluto/Zorn/issues/4)

<Separator/>

A solution was provided in the issue.  
It now works correctly.
