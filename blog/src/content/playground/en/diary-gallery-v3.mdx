## A. Airmail postcards

Postcards on a low map/kraft-paper desk. Clicking a card flips the postcard in 3D inside the lightbox (front = photo, back = handwritten letter).

<PostcardGallery client:visible items={sample} />

## B. Vintage film / slides

A 35mm film strip with sprocket holes and sepia tones. Clicking a frame enlarges it into a slide mount on a light table, with a handwritten mount label. The mount tilts slightly with mouse movement.

<FilmStripGallery client:visible items={sample} />

## C. Travel journal

Photos attached with washi tape to the open pages of a bound journal. Clicking a photo lifts the page in 3D and displays a handwritten note in the margin.

<JournalGallery client:visible items={sample} />

## D. Velocity-linked 3D stream

A technique from [Motion’s scroll-velocity example](https://examples.motion.dev/react/scroll-velocity-linked-offset). A 3D stream of photos arranged diagonally with depth. **It flows as you scroll**, and scroll velocity (`useVelocity`) changes card tilt and offset in real time like a wave. Clicking a card enlarges it in a lightbox with a handwritten caption.

<VelocityStream client:visible items={sample} />
